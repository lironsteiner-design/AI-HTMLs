KATE KIE Agentic Temporary Works Engineer
Prompt guide

Search past temporary works with KATE.

Use KATE to find relevant examples, people, risks, and lessons from past temporary works projects.

Tips for refining or speeding up the search

Limit the search

  • “Within the past [X] years”
  • “In [state / region]”
  • “Only search through a handful of relevant tasks”
  • “Limit the search so it doesn’t take too long”

Make it more specific

  • “Search in [X] files within the QC folder”
  • Specify the task category, table, or file type.

Similar tasks and specific info

Start with precedent when you need an example, package, or kickoff view.

  • “Show me falsework category 511.03 calc packages from the past few years”
  • “Using the five most similar past projects, prepare a design kickoff brief for a new temporary working surface. Highlight commonalities and risks to consider”

Look for experts

Locate people with relevant experience.

  • “Who has worked on high-risk bridge demos as the EOR or designer?”
  • “Find designers or EORs with experience on similar temporary works projects and summarize the relevant work.”

Test assumptions

Pressure-test a design assumption against past practice.

  • “I am assuming a uniform load for this load case, is there an example of this being done in a past trestle design?”
  • “Compare this assumption with similar past projects and flag where the precedent supports or differs.”

Understand risks and concerns

Surface concerns and learn about past challenges.

  • “For falsework systems, what are the key risks generally identified in the QRSD form within the QC folder.”
  • “Find projects where the temporary works design changed between the calculation package, shop drawings, and field installation. Summarize the changes and why they occurred.”
