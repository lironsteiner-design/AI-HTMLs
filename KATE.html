Prompt categories

Choose the question you need to answer, then make it specific.

Find similar tasks + specific info

Start with precedent when you need an example, package, or kickoff view.

  • “Show me timber falsework calc packages from the past few years and list the designer, EOR, and state.”
  • “Using the five most similar past projects, prepare a design kickoff brief for a new temporary working surface. Include likely load cases, coordination questions, and risks to consider.”

Find experts

Locate people with relevant project, design, or EOR experience.

  • “Who has worked on high-risk bridge demos as the EOR or designer?”
  • “Find designers or EORs with experience on similar temporary works projects and summarize the relevant work.”

Test assumptions

Pressure-test a design assumption against past practice.

  • “I’m assuming a uniform load for [topic]. Is there an example of this being done in a trestle design?”
  • “Compare this assumption with similar past projects and flag where the precedent supports or differs.”

Understand risks + concerns

Surface recurring concerns and learn where designs changed during delivery.

  • “For falsework systems, what are the key risks generally identified in QRSD forms within the QC folder?”
  • “Find projects where the temporary works design changed between the calculation package, shop drawings, and field installation. Summarize the changes and why they occurred.”

Refine or speed up the search

Add one or two details when the first search is too broad.

  • Task category / table
  • Folder or file type
  • Search in [X] files
  • Within the past [X] years
  • In [state / region]
  • Give [X] examples
  • Only relevant tasks
  • Limit the search time
